Hello, I'm Sakshi 👋

Welcome to Beyond The Obvious.

I started this space because I like questioning the things we often accept without thinking — education, careers, skills, ambition, work, creativity, and the way we look at life.

Sometimes, a simple story can make us see a bigger problem. Sometimes, one small idea can make us look at our own choices differently.

That's what I want this blog to be about.

I'll share stories, ideas, personal experiences, and things worth thinking about — honestly, simply, and without trying to make everything sound complicated.

I'm still learning, figuring things out, and building my own path too. So this isn't a place where I pretend to have all the answers.

It's a place to think, question, learn, and look beyond what's obvious.

Thanks for being here. 💛

— Sakshi
